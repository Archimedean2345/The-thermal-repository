# Current Programs At DynX-HPR
First release: october 11, 2025
Last update: october 11, 2025

Ignis & Flammam are the programs for research and development of solid rocket motors and liquid fuel engines.
Flammam was the first R&D program, the main objective was to break the barrier of unknowledge of SRM in the region Monterrey, Nuevo León.

A serial of three designs were developed:
"Lucia" our first SRM; a motor-probe for testing combustion behavior and measuring thermodinamical parameters of our interest, but it was not succesfully manufactured. All of the work here was achived thankfully to the sheared knowledge of Richard Nakka, canadian Engineer and Rocketeer. All the references can be find across the repository on each tool used or developed.

The subsequent SRM was "Chorrillo"; our most powerfull motor, class K with average thrust of almost 1300 Newtons. This motor was specifically design for mission "Promesa", launched in Laguna de Sayula in contest ENMICE 2025, Jalisco and several test near Nuevo León.

The latest motor is "Replicante", an easy-to-manufacture SRM for academic purpose and forward studies. Currently being developed.

The next step is Ignis, pretends to develop and manufacture the second liquid fuel engine in México. This program is going to be developed on 2026 and leave the engine for forward studies for the team.
